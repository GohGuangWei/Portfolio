Hi! My name is Goh Guang Wei. I'm dedicated to bring clean and clear designs for users, hoping to satisfy their needs while achieving their goals.


I am a CS-grad developer who loves building ideas and making them work while looking cool at the same time. Since 2018, through tinkering with Python and Discord bots, I found my love of programming and the satisfaction of making things work; since then, I have been building non-stop, whether that's knowledge or solutions that are crucially needed for the public to move their days along.


During college, I have experienced numerous cases of dark patterns applied within designs, bringing forth displeasure on my use of them, which got me thinking: why do people develop such designs, if their intentions are to keep them entertained? That curiosity got me diving deeper into the world of UI/UX, which ignited my resolve to make use of my current skills to provide the best design possible for all users. Self-taught, I strive to create designs that are eye-catching yet catered to user needs, without the need of annoyances we all surely have experienced before.


When I am not designing, I do love to draw whatever is on my mind in my sketchbook, then digitalize them with a drawing tablet. I also like playing Commander in Magic: The Gathering, and a big fan of Porter Robinson’s music. (Feel free to talk to me about them!)
